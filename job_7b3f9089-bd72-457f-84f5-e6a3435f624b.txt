About the Role

Reporting to the Director of GTM Enablement & Programs, the GTM Enablement Specialist will support the execution of training, content, and tools that enable our Sales and Customer Success teams to perform at a high level. This role is ideal for someone early in their career looking to grow in GTM operations or enablement within a fast-paced, high-growth SaaS environment.

Responsibilities

• Support onboarding programs for new Sales and Customer Success team members by managing logistics, tracking completion, and maintaining training content

• Maintain and organize enablement materials such as playbooks, messaging decks, onboarding guides, and certification tracks

• Assist in the creation and delivery of ongoing enablement initiatives such as product training, methodology sessions, and sales skill workshops

• Manage scheduling, communications, and follow-up activities for all internal training programs

• Maintain and support the enablement platform (e.g., Showpad & Magentrix LMS), ensuring content is current, accessible, and well-organized

• Track and report on enablement program participation and impact metrics (e.g., onboarding progress, tool adoption, training completions)

• Serve as a liaison between Revenue Operations and field teams to gather feedback, identify needs, and implement improvements

• Stay current on Keeper’s platform and GTM strategies to ensure enablement materials stay relevant and aligned

Requirements

• 1+ years of experience in enablement, sales operations, or a related support function within a B2B SaaS environment

• Familiarity with GTM motions across Sales and CSM teams

• Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously

• Strong verbal and written communication skills; able to work cross-functionally with different stakeholder groups

• Experience with enablement tools (e.g., Showpad, LMS), CRMs like Salesforce, and content management platforms preferred

• Preferred working knowledge of Identity and Access Management (IAM) and Privileged Access Management (PAM), including core concepts and real-world application

• Self-starter mindset with the ability to operate in a fast-paced, evolving environment

• Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or a related field preferred
